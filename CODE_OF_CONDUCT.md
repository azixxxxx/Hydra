# Code of conduct

Hydra contributors must keep technical discussion respectful, specific, and evidence-based. Critique code, design, tests, and claims rather than people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, credential disclosure, and publication of another person's private model transcripts or account data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users. Report conduct issues privately to the repository maintainers after publication. Do not use a public issue when a report contains personal, security, or account information.
